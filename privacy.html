BabaShuo Stats: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Last updated: November 12, 2026

BabaShuo Stats is a private, read-only reporting tool. It is used only by the owner of the BabaShuo brand (the BabaShuo Etsy shop, YouTube channel, TikTok account and Pinterest account) to collect statistics about their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects one of their own accounts, the app reads, with the owner's permission:

  • YouTube: channel and video statistics (views, likes, comments, watch time, subscribers).
  • TikTok: basic profile information and video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, follower count).
  • Pinterest: account analytics and the owner's own pins' statistics (impressions, saves, clicks, video views).
  • Etsy: the owner's shop details, listing views and favorites, and order totals.

The app does not access data of other users, does not read private messages, and does not post, edit or delete anything on any platform.

How the data is used and stored

Once a week the app saves summary numbers (for example, weekly views per video) to the owner's private Airtable base, so the owner can track how the brand is doing. Login tokens are stored on the owner's private automation server and are used only to fetch these statistics.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Only the owner can see it.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in each platform's account settings, which stops all access. Saved statistics can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Terms of Service

  • BabaShuo Stats is a personal tool for the owner's own accounts only. No other users are permitted.
  • The app only reads statistics; it does not create, change or remove content on any platform.
  • The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
  • Use of each platform through this app is also subject to that platform's own terms (YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest and Etsy).
